Set up in three steps

No account and nothing else to install. aiHelper runs on your PC and talks to the AI with your keys.

  1. Install

    Run aiHelper-Setup.exe. Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” because the app isn’t signed with a paid certificate: click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Add your keys

    aiHelper opens ⚙ Settings on first start. Paste:

    • Groq: free, hears the meeting fastest
    • Gemini: free, backup for hearing
    • Claude: writes the answers (or choose Gemini for free answers)

  3. Start a call

    In Start a call, type the role and company, upload your resume and add a few lines about you and the job. Join your meeting and answers appear as questions are asked. Press End call for notes.

What it does

Everything happens in one small overlay you can drag anywhere. Clicks pass through it except on its buttons.

Hears the meeting

Picks up what the other people say through your computer’s sound, even with headphones.

Answers in about 2–3 seconds

When a question is asked, an answer streams in. Or press Ctrl Enter any time.

Reads your screen

Ctrl Shift Enter answers what’s on screen: a coding problem, a slide, a form.

Code that reads like code

Programming answers come as coloured code blocks with a short explanation.

Notes after every call

A summary, the questions asked and follow-ups, plus the full transcript and “Ask AI” about the call.

Practice interviews

An AI interviewer asks questions out loud and scores your answers.

Your keys and calls stay yours

  • Keys are saved only on your PC, in %APPDATA%\aiHelper. The download contains no keys.
  • aiHelper only answers to your own computer: not other devices, not websites.
  • During a call, speech and screenshots go to the AI services you chose, to be written down and answered.
Is it free?

The app is free. Groq and Gemini have free plans that cover hearing your meetings. Claude answers are paid by whoever owns the Claude key.

Why does Windows warn me?

Apps without a paid code-signing certificate show “Windows protected your PC” the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. aiHelper is for Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11.

Can others see the overlay when I share my screen?

No. Windows leaves the overlay out of screen sharing and screenshots. Do a quick test share in your meeting app to see it for yourself.

How do I update?

Download and run the latest installer from this page. Your settings and sessions are kept.
